# YMCA Registration Process Improvement

A Lean Six Sigma process improvement project analyzing new-member registration times to identify factors associated with process delays and develop data-driven recommendations.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project was to analyze the YMCA new-member registration process, identify factors associated with longer registration times, and recommend opportunities to improve operational efficiency.

## Data

The analysis included 40 new-member registration observations collected across seven operating days.

The outcome variable was **New Member Registration Time (minutes)**.

Factors analyzed included:
- Document readiness
- Staff experience
- Time of day
- Membership type
- Payment method

## Methods

- Descriptive statistics
- Data visualization
- Two-sample t-tests
- One-way ANOVA
- Process mapping
- Fishbone analysis
- Lean Six Sigma process improvement

## Key Findings

Four factors showed statistically significant relationships with registration time:

- **Document readiness:** Registrations with missing or incomplete documents took 4.76 minutes longer on average (p = 0.00045).
- **Staff experience:** Registrations handled by new staff took 2.93 minutes longer on average (p = 0.00783).
- **Time of day:** Evening registrations had the longest average registration time at 21.24 minutes (p = 0.0000723).
- **Membership type:** Family memberships had the longest average registration time at 20.59 minutes (p = 0.00209).

Payment method was not statistically significant (p = 0.48531).

## Business Impact

The baseline average registration time was **18.22 minutes**.

The project established a target of reducing average registration time by **20%**, from **18.22 minutes to 14.6 minutes**.

If the target is achieved, the improvement is estimated to create approximately **108.6 hours of staff capacity annually**.

## Recommendations

Improvement efforts should focus on:
- Improving document readiness before registration
- Supporting and training newer staff
- Addressing delays during evening periods
- Streamlining Family membership registrations
